Support

Contact Famni

Email team@beautyai.app with the subject “Famni Support”. Include your app version, iPhone model, iOS version, a description of the issue and approximate time. Do not send passwords, full payment details, invitation secrets or precise location histories.

Location and alerts

Check your sharing mode, iOS location permission, notification permission, connectivity and battery settings. A stale timestamp means the location may be outdated. Paused sharing, restricted permissions or OS limits can stop updates. Family alerts are not emergency dispatch.

Subscriptions and restoration

Open the plan screen to restore purchases with the same Apple Account. Manage or cancel in iPhone Settings > your name > Subscriptions. Deleting Famni or your account does not cancel billing. Refunds are handled by Apple. A Famni Family subscription covers one designated Famni family, separately from Apple Family Sharing.

Privacy and account help

Use Account Deletion for verified requests. You can also export data, delete history and revoke devices from the app. Contact us about abuse, an unfamiliar session or an account you cannot access.

Before release

Famni is in preparation for release. Production authentication, billing and physical-device delivery verification are still release requirements.
