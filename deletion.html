Account Deletion & Privacy Choices

Delete in the app

Open Famni > Settings > Account and choose account deletion. If you own a family, transfer ownership to another eligible member first. You can separately delete location history and request an export in Settings.

Request help by email

Email team@beautyai.app with the subject “Famni Account Deletion” from the address associated with your account. If you use Sign in with Apple and Hide My Email, include the account relay email shown in Famni and explain that you use Apple sign-in. We will verify control of the account before deleting information; never send your password or Apple credentials. You may request deletion assistance even if you cannot access the app.

What deletion covers

Verified deletion removes account-linked data through the account deletion workflow and revokes sessions. Minimal deletion tombstones prevent deleted data from being restored into active service. Provider revocation can require retries. Earlier backup copies remain until backup expiry. Legally required billing, fraud and dispute records may be retained for the applicable period. We will explain any retention that applies to your request.

Sharing and billing

You can pause or change location precision, leave a family, block or report a member and revoke devices independently of deleting your account. Deletion cannot erase information already copied by other members. Deletion does not cancel Apple subscriptions; cancel separately in your Apple Account settings.

Other privacy requests

For access, correction, export, restriction, objection or consent questions, email team@beautyai.app with “Famni Privacy Request”. We respond to verified requests within the time required by applicable law.
